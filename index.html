Section 1

Employee Relations

We're committed to fostering an outstanding relationship with every member of our team.

Equal Employment Opportunity

Stashdash Delivery is committed to equal employment opportunities for all employees and applicants without regard to race, ethnicity, religion, color, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, national origin, ancestry, age, disability, pregnancy, marital status, veteran status, or any other protected characteristic under applicable federal, state, and local laws.

This policy extends to all aspects of employment: recruiting, hiring, discipline, termination, promotions, transfers, compensation, benefits, training, and all other terms and conditions of employment.

Anti-Harassment & Discrimination

The Company is committed to maintaining a safe and respectful workplace free from unlawful harassment, discrimination, bullying, gossip, and retaliation. This policy applies to the workplace, employer-sponsored events, and social media.

Harassment includes: verbal abuse, slurs, jokes, or ridicule based on protected characteristics; unwelcome sexual advances; physical conduct of a threatening or humiliating nature; distribution of offensive graphic material.

⚠ Zero Tolerance

Any employee found to have violated this policy will be subject to discipline, up to and including termination. This includes supervisors and managers.

Reasonable Accommodations

The Company will provide reasonable accommodations for known physical or mental disabilities and for employees' sincerely held religious beliefs and practices, provided the accommodation does not create undue hardship or a direct safety threat. Contact your POC to begin the accommodation process.

Section 2

Code of Conduct & Ethics

We all deserve to work in an environment of dignity and respect. Every employee is expected to uphold these standards.

Ethical Business Practices

Employees must uphold the highest standards of integrity and honesty in all business interactions. This means truthfulness, transparency, and sound ethical decision-making. You must promptly disclose any potential conflicts of interest that could compromise the Company's integrity.

⚠ Immediate Termination Offenses

Providing false, dishonest, or misleading information on any Company document, application, time entry, or to any customer, vendor, or colleague.

Engaging in any illegal activity while on duty, on Company property, or off-duty conduct that reflects negatively on the Company.

Professionalism

All employees are expected to conduct themselves with the highest degree of professionalism — respectful and courteous demeanor, appropriate language, and proper handling of confidential information. Rudeness, profanity, or disruptive conduct will not be tolerated.

Conflicts of Interest

Employees are prohibited from engaging in any business that competes with the Company, or holding a financial interest in a competitor. If you believe your outside activities may create a conflict, notify your POC immediately.

Anti-Fraternization

No person in a management or supervisory position may have a romantic relationship with an employee they directly supervise. Employees in the same department should also avoid such relationships. If a relationship develops, it must be immediately disclosed to your POC.

Company Events Conduct

At all Company-sponsored events, employees must stay with their team, maintain professional conduct, and adhere to all Company policies. Employees are strictly prohibited from driving after consuming any alcohol at a Company event — a designated driver or rideshare is required.

Internal Promotions

Promotions are based on performance, conduct, and role requirements. To be eligible, employees must have a satisfactory performance record and no disciplinary actions in the preceding 6 months. No promotion will be based on favoritism, nepotism, fraternization, or discrimination.

Discipline & Attendance

Clear standards, fair process. The Company uses a progressive discipline model designed to help employees correct issues and improve.

Progressive Discipline Model

Each category — attendance, performance, and misconduct — progresses through its own separate disciplinary track. The Company reserves the right to skip steps based on severity.

Level 1
Verbal Warning
Level 2
Written Warning
Level 3
Final Warning
Level 4
Termination
Introductory Period (first 90 days): The discipline track is accelerated — Written Warning → Final Warning → Termination. Employees within the introductory period are held to a higher standard and may be released at any time.
Attendance Definitions
Absence
1 unexcused, unprotected absence = 1 occurrence
Tardy
2 tardies = 1 occurrence. Clocking in after your scheduled start time.
Early Departure
2 unexcused early departures = 1 occurrence
No Call No Show
Failure to communicate about a missed shift. Treated as a serious violation.
Call-In Procedure

If you will be absent or tardy, call your on-duty manager or use the call-out line at least 3 hours before your shift. Failure to follow this procedure may result in disciplinary action.

Job Abandonment

Failing to report to work for 3 or more consecutive scheduled shifts without notifying management will be considered voluntary resignation.

Misconduct — Immediate Termination Examples
  • ✕Theft or fraud
  • ✕Physical violence or threats of violence
  • ✕Insubordination
  • ✕Harassment or discrimination
  • ✕Delivering cannabis to an underage customer
  • ✕Unauthorized sharing of Company IP or confidential information
  • ✕Falsifying time records or Company documents

Driver Safety

Your safety is the top priority always. No delivery, no product, no amount of cash is worth compromising it.

Non-Negotiable Safety Rules
!

!

!
Never leave your vehicle if multiple unknown individuals approach
!
If a situation feels unsafe — leave immediately and call your POC
!
Personal safety always takes priority over product or cash
Vehicle Positioning
⊙
Park passenger-side facing the delivery
Shorter exposure distance from vehicle to the door.
↑
Plan your exit before you get out
Avoid positions that require backing into traffic to leave.
◎
Well-lit, visible areas only
No dark alleys, dead ends, or tight spots you can't easily leave.
⊘
Never block driveways or fire lanes
Stay clear of traffic. Keep space to pull away fast.
Stay Alert
  • ✓Scan the full area before exiting the vehicle — every single time
  • ✓Be aware of anyone loitering nearby before you approach
  • ✓Trust your instincts — if something feels wrong, it probably is. Leave and call your POC.
  • ✕No headphones during deliveries — you need full situational awareness
  • ✕No prolonged phone use outside the vehicle

Company Vehicles & Safe Driving

You must hold a valid state driver's license for the class of vehicle you are driving. Seatbelts are mandatory for all persons in Company vehicles at all times. Never use a Company vehicle while under the influence of any substance.

⚠ DUI / Reckless Driving

Any employee convicted of DUI/DWI or reckless driving while operating a Company vehicle will be considered to have an unacceptable driving record and continued employment will be subject to review.

Delivery Operations · Section 2

Age Verification & Compliance

California law is clear. These rules are non-negotiable — there are no exceptions, no matter how many times you've delivered to that customer.

The Golden Rules — No Exceptions, Ever
✕
No valid government-issued ID = No delivery. Full stop.
✕
Expired ID = No delivery.
✕
Person doesn't match the ID = No delivery.
✕
Customer is under 21 = No delivery (unless specific medical regulations apply).
✕
Regular customer you've delivered to 100 times = Still check the ID.
What to Verify on Every ID
Date of Birth
Customer must be 21 or older. Do the math — don't assume.
Photo Match
Does the face in front of you match the ID photo? When in doubt, do not deliver.
Expiration Date
Expired IDs are not valid — even expired by one day.
Type of ID
Government-issued only: driver's license, passport, or state ID. No other forms accepted.
Scenario: A regular customer answers the door. She's placed 30 orders. You recognize her. She can't find her ID.

Answer: No delivery. Familiarity and order history do not matter. Apologize, explain the policy, and return the product per Company procedure. You will never be disciplined for following this rule.
The Delivery Sequence
  • 1Before approaching: Confirm the address, review customer notes, ensure order is secured in approved bag
  • 2At the door: Verify customer identity first — before presenting the product
  • 3Complete compliance: Finish all required compliance steps before handing over anything
  • 4Payment: Follow all payment procedures exactly
  • 5Failed delivery: Do NOT leave product unattended. Do NOT leave it with neighbors, roommates, or family members who are not the verified customer. Return it.
Scenario: A customer's roommate answers the door. The actual customer stepped out. The roommate shows you their own valid ID and says they'll take the order.

Answer: Do not deliver. The product can only be handed to the verified customer on the order — not anyone else, regardless of their own ID status. Take the product back.

Delivery Operations · Section 3

Product Security

From the moment you leave the store, every product assigned to you is your responsibility. Concealment and chain of custody are non-negotiable.

Concealment Rules
  • ✕Never place cannabis products on the front seat, dashboard, or passenger floorboard
  • ✕Never leave products visible to the public from outside the vehicle
  • ✕Never discuss delivery contents with anyone other than the verified customer
  • ✓Use only Company-approved locked bags, totes, or storage compartments
  • ✓Orders must remain concealed and secured at all times during transport
Vehicle Security
  • ✓Lock the vehicle every single time you step away — even for 30 seconds
  • ✓Never leave products in an unlocked vehicle
  • ✓Verify your order contents before leaving the store or dispatch location
  • ✕Never leave cannabis products unattended under any circumstances

Chain of Custody: You are responsible for all products assigned to you from the moment you receive them until delivery is complete or the product is returned. Report any discrepancies immediately — do not wait until end of shift.

Scenario: You're running multiple stops. After a quick handoff, you realize your bag is sitting on the front passenger seat in plain view as you drive to the next address.

Answer: Pull over safely and transfer the product to your approved locked bag or storage compartment immediately before continuing. It takes 30 seconds — it's always worth it.

Delivery Operations · Section 4

Cash Handling & Robbery Response

Product and cash can be replaced. You cannot. Always prioritize your safety above everything else.

Cash Security
  • ✓Keep cash separated and secured at all times
  • ✓Count cash discreetly — never in public view
  • ✓Follow the Company's cash-limit policy
  • ✕Never display or flash large amounts of cash in public
If You Are Robbed
Robbery Response Protocol
1
Do NOT resist. Product and cash can be replaced. Your life cannot.
2
Observe as many details as you safely can — clothing, direction, vehicle description.
3
Get to safety first. Leave the area if you can do so safely.
4
Call 911 if you were threatened or a weapon was shown.
5
Call your POC as soon as it is safe to do so.
Call 911 When:
You are threatened or a weapon is shown
A vehicle accident occurs
A medical emergency requires an ambulance
Call POC When:
Any robbery attempt (weapon or not)
Suspicious customers or situations
Product discrepancies
Any law enforcement interaction
Vehicle accidents (also 911)
Device failures
Scenario: Someone approaches your vehicle aggressively and demands the product and cash. No weapon is shown.

Answer: Comply. Do not resist. Get safe, then immediately call 911 if applicable and your POC. Your personal safety is the only thing that matters in that moment.

Delivery Operations · Section 5

Professionalism & Customer Service

You represent Stashdash Delivery every time you're on the road. Brief, respectful, professional — every interaction.

Customer Interaction Standards
  • ✓Be courteous, respectful, and keep interactions brief
  • ✓Maintain a clean and professional appearance on every shift
  • ✕Never accept invitations into a customer's home
  • ✕Never argue with or confront a customer — disengage professionally and contact your POC
What You May Never Discuss with Customers
⚠ Do Not Share These

Other customers' orders or information

Your delivery route or remaining stops

Current inventory levels or what's in stock

Cash amounts you are carrying

Internal Company operations, staffing, or pricing

Friendly small talk is perfectly fine. Anything on this list — even a casual comment — is not. When in doubt, keep it brief and move on.

Technology on Deliveries
  • ✓Keep your delivery device charged before every shift
  • ✓Follow GPS and route procedures as directed
  • ✓Report any discrepancies or device issues immediately
  • ✕No earbuds or headphones during active deliveries — you need full awareness
  • ✕No texting, emailing, or phone use while driving — pull over safely first
Scenario: A customer asks how many stops you have left tonight and whether you're usually in their neighborhood.

Answer: Give a friendly, vague response — "Just making the rounds!" — and wrap up the interaction. Never share route, schedule, or operational information with customers.
Scenario: A customer is upset about their order and starts raising their voice at the door.

Answer: Stay calm, stay respectful, and keep the interaction brief. Do not argue. If the behavior continues, politely disengage and contact your POC to report the situation.

Safety & Security · Section 1

Workplace Safety

Safety is everyone's responsibility. Report unsafe conditions, cooperate with safety inspections, and always think safety first.

Safety Policy

The health and well-being of our employees is foremost among the Company's concerns. All accidents — including minor ones — must be reported immediately to your supervisor. Consult the Company's Injury and Illness Prevention Program (IIPP) for additional detail.

Weapons in the Workplace

The Company strictly prohibits all employees from possessing any weapons on Company premises, in Company vehicles, or in personal vehicles used for delivery services — regardless of concealed weapons permits.

Prohibited
Guns, tasers, air guns, pellet guns
Prohibited
Knives, switchblades, daggers, swords
Prohibited
Explosives, nightsticks
Prohibited
Any object defined as a dangerous weapon by law

Pepper spray exception: Employees may carry personal pepper spray while not on Company premises, so long as it is used lawfully and not in the course of performing employment duties.

Workplace Violence

The Company has a zero-tolerance policy for violent acts or threats of violence against employees, customers, or vendors. Never confront someone you suspect is carrying a concealed weapon — contact your Safety & Security department immediately.

Theft

Theft of money or property from the Company, co-workers, or customers will result in immediate termination and referral to law enforcement. The Company reserves the right to inspect any vehicle, bag, locker, or personal belonging on Company property to investigate theft allegations.

Safety & Security · Section 2

Alcohol, Drugs & Smoking

You are operating a vehicle and handling regulated products. Impairment of any kind is a serious safety risk and a serious policy violation.

Drug & Alcohol Policy

All employees are prohibited from manufacturing, distributing, possessing, or using illegal drugs or marijuana (regardless of prescription) on Company property, in Company vehicles, or while performing work duties away from Company premises.

Employees are also prohibited from having unauthorized controlled substances or marijuana in their system while at work, or from having excessive amounts of otherwise lawful substances in their systems.

⚠ Critical for Delivery Drivers

It is strictly forbidden to operate any vehicle while under the influence of any substance — including legally prescribed medication that causes dizziness or drowsiness. If your prescription may affect your ability to drive, disclose it to your POC immediately.

Smoking

Smoking — including e-cigarettes, vapes, and personal vaporizing devices — is prohibited in all Company buildings and vehicles. Smoking must be confined to designated outdoor areas only.

Drug & Alcohol Testing

Employees may be subject to drug or alcohol screening if the Company has reasonable suspicion of a violation. Reasonable suspicion may arise from supervisory observation, co-worker reports, performance decline, behavioral changes, or involvement in a work-related accident.

Employees in safety-sensitive positions (including drivers) may be tested on a random or periodic basis as permitted by applicable law. Refusing to cooperate with testing, tampering with a sample, or failing to execute consent forms may result in termination.

Safety & Security · Section 3

Technology & Social Media

Company devices are for Company business. Protect confidential information and use good judgment online.

Device Usage

Company-provided devices are to be used exclusively for work-related tasks. Personal activities including texting, streaming, or browsing non-work websites are prohibited during work hours. Unless preapproved by management, earbuds and headsets are not permitted during working hours.

Driving & Devices

Employees who drive as part of their duties are expected to refrain from using personal devices while driving. Regardless of circumstances — including slow or stopped traffic — pull over safely before placing or accepting a call or texting. Traffic violations incurred from personal device use are the sole liability of the employee.

No Expectation of Privacy

All Company IT resources, communications systems, and devices are Company property. Employees have no expectation of privacy when using Company systems. The Company reserves the right to monitor, intercept, and review all electronic activities on Company systems.

Social Media

Employees are personally responsible for what they communicate on social media. Do not post anything that brings the Company into disrepute or discloses confidential information. If you disclose your affiliation with Stashdash Delivery, include a disclaimer that your views are your own and do not represent the Company.

⚠ Social Media Violations

Using social media to harass, discriminate, disclose confidential information, or misrepresent the Company may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

Photography & Video

Employees must not photograph or record in restricted areas, share confidential information through photos or videos, or invade the privacy of other employees while on Company premises or during work hours.

Employment · Section 1

Employment Terms

Understanding the terms of your employment is important. Here's what you need to know.

Introductory Period

The first 90 days of employment is an introductory period during which your performance, attendance, and overall attitude will be assessed. Employees who do not demonstrate expected performance and commitment may be terminated during or upon completion of this period.

Completion of the introductory period does not change the at-will employment relationship. The Company may extend your introductory period if additional assessment time is needed.

At-Will Employment

Employment at Stashdash Delivery is at-will. This means either you or the Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without notice, and with or without cause. Nothing in this handbook creates a promise of continued employment.

Background Screening

The Company reserves the right to conduct background screening on all employees to ensure qualifications, safety, and suitability. Contact your POC with any questions.

Resignations

We ask employees to provide at least 2 weeks' written notice before resigning. Return all Company property including ID badges, keys, and equipment before your last day. You may be asked to attend an exit interview.

Employee Classification
Full-Time
Normally scheduled 30+ hours per week.
Part-Time
Normally scheduled fewer than 30 hours per week.
Temporary
Seasonal or special projects. Not eligible for Company benefits unless required by law.

Employment · Section 2

Timekeeping & Payroll

Accurate timekeeping is required by law and ensures you get paid correctly. Working off the clock is never allowed.

Timekeeping Rules
  • ✓Clock in and out via the Time Clock portal at the start and end of every shift
  • ✓Clock out for all unpaid meal periods
  • ✓Report time entry errors immediately via the employee portal
  • ✕Working "off the clock" is strictly prohibited
  • ✕Never record another employee's time
  • ✕Overtime without prior supervisor approval may result in disciplinary action
California Break Requirements

Non-exempt employees who work more than 5 hours must take an uninterrupted 30-minute meal period (no later than the end of the 5th hour). A second 30-minute meal period is required if you work more than 10 hours.

Hours Worked in DayPaid Rest Breaks
At least 3.5 and up to 6 hours1
More than 6 and up to 10 hours2
More than 10 and up to 14 hours3

Rest periods are paid — you do not clock out for them. They are 10 minutes and you are relieved of all duties. No supervisor may instruct you to skip a rest period.

Report any instruction to skip a meal or rest period to management immediately at alex.sanchez@stashdash.com.
